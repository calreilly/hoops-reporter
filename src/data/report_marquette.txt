Marquette Golden Eagles Injury Update

Marquette faces a difficult path following a series of nagging injuries. Star guard Tyler Kolek is recovering from an oblique strain. While he is cleared to play, his burst driving to the basket appears compromised. Head coach Shaka Smart has adjusted by implementing more half-court sets rather than relying purely on transition offense.

In recent games against dropping defenses, Marquette has struggled to hit the open three. If drawn into a half-court rock fight against a strong defensive rebounding team like UConn, Marquette's lack of second-chance points becomes glaring.
