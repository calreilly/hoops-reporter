UConn Huskies Season Overview - Big East Briefing

The UConn Huskies continue to be a powerhouse under Dan Hurley. Their offensive system relies on constant off-ball movement, punishing back-door cuts, and high-level passing from both their guards and big men. Donovan Clingan has been dominant, logging a season-high 18 rebounds in a physical matchup against Marquette last week.

The Huskies utilize a suffocating man-to-man defense. Their primary weakness this season has been defending highly mobile, shooting big men who pull Clingan away from the paint. Guard Tristen Newton's leadership remains critical; when he limits turnovers, UConn is historically undefeated.
