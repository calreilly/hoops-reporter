Purdue Boilermakers Scouting Report - March 2026

The Purdue Boilermakers continue to run their offense through their dominant interior presence, but have expanded their perimeter shooting. Point guard Braden Smith is the engine, averaging 13.5 points and 8.2 assists per game. However, Smith suffered a minor ankle sprain during practice this week. Head coach Matt Painter stated he is day-to-day but is expected to start in the Sweet 16.

Defensively, Purdue struggles against teams that employ a heavy pick-and-pop scheme, as their bigs prefer drop coverage. In their upset loss to Fairleigh Dickinson in a past tournament, FDU utilized relentless full-court pressure and perimeter spacing. Teams looking to beat Purdue must force turnovers and hit contested threes.
